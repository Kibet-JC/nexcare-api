## 2. The information we collect

We only collect information that is necessary to provide your care or to meet a legal obligation.

### 2.1 Information you give us

- **Identity:** full name, date of birth, sex, national ID or passport number where required for SHA, insurance, or statutory records.
- **Contact:** phone number, email address, physical address, next of kin.
- **Clinical:** medical history, presenting complaints, examination findings, diagnoses, treatments, prescriptions, lab and imaging results, allergies, vaccinations.
- **Financial:** payment method, SHA / SHIF membership, private insurance details, billing history.
- **Other:** anything else you choose to share with your clinician.

### 2.2 Information we collect automatically

- Appointment metadata (date, time, location, clinician seen).
- Service usage data (which features you use in the patient portal).
- Technical data (IP address, browser type) for security and audit purposes.

### 2.3 Information we receive from others

- Referrals, lab and imaging results from partner facilities.
- Eligibility responses from SHA and private insurers (where applicable).

## 4. Who we share your information with

We do not sell your data. We share it only where necessary, and only with parties bound by appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations.

- **Clinicians inside NexCare** who are involved in your care.
- **Partner facilities** when you are referred for tests, imaging, or specialist care.
- **Social Health Authority (SHA / SHIF)** and **private insurers** for eligibility checks and claims.
- **Statutory bodies** (e.g., Ministry of Health) for legally required reporting.
- **Service providers (sub-processors)** who help us run NexCare — for example, hosting, backups, and AI services. The current list is in `COMPLIANCE.md`.
- **Law enforcement or courts** where we are legally required to disclose.

We never share your data for purposes you have not been informed of.

## 7. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

- **Be informed** about how we use your data (this notice).
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct** data that is inaccurate or out of date.
- **Object** to processing in certain circumstances.
- **Request deletion** of data, where the law allows.
- **Data portability** — receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, where consent is the lawful basis.
- **Lodge a complaint** with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

To exercise any of these rights, write to _TODO: privacy@nexcare-domain_. We will respond within the timelines set out in the KDPA and its regulations. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

You also have the right under the **Health Act, 2017** to access information in your health record. Your clinician can guide you through this process.

## 8. How we keep your information safe

We use technical and organizational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data, including:

- Encryption of clinical data at rest and in transit.
- Role-based access control — only people who need to see your record can see it.
- An append-only audit trail of every access and change.
- Automated, encrypted backups with tested restore procedures.
- Regular security reviews and dependency updates.
- Staff training and confidentiality obligations.

If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in line with our obligations under the KDPA. The full incident-response playbook is in `SECURITY.md`.
